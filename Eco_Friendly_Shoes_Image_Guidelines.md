## VISUAL BRAND DIRECTION

### Color Palette
- **Primary Greens:** Sage green, forest green, olive
- **Earth Tones:** Tan, beige, natural brown
- **Accent Colors:** Neon lime, coral, or electric blue (for pop)
- **Neutrals:** White, off-white, charcoal

### Aesthetic Principles
- ✅ Authentic & raw (not overly polished)
- ✅ Modern & minimalist
- ✅ Editorial photography style
- ✅ Gen Z authentic vibes
- ✅ High-end luxury sustainable brand feel
- ❌ Avoid: Corporate, generic, preachy tone

### Photography Style
- Documentary-style realism
- Soft, natural lighting
- Street style editorial quality
- Film grain or 35mm aesthetic where appropriate
- High contrast, vibrant yet earthy tones

## IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS

### PROMPT 1: PRODUCT HERO SHOT

**Use For:** Primary product showcase, website hero, main marketing asset

```
Ultra-modern eco-friendly sneaker in vibrant earth-tone colorway (sage green + natural tan), 
shot from a 3/4 angle on a clean white background. Shoe features visible sustainable details: 
recycled material texture, plant-based sole, minimal branding. Soft, professional studio lighting 
with subtle shadow. Photorealistic, high-end product photography style. Minimal, clean aesthetic. 
No people. 8K resolution. Fashion magazine quality.
```

**Variants:**
- Change colorway: "forest green + charcoal" or "earth brown + cream"
- Add detail: "showing recycled plastic and cork sole"
- Adjust lighting: "golden hour side lighting" or "dramatic studio lighting"

---

### PROMPT 2: LIFESTYLE PRODUCT SHOT

**Use For:** Social media, Instagram feed, lifestyle content

```
Eco-friendly sustainable sneaker worn by a young person (Gen Z aesthetic) walking through 
urban environment. Shoe close-up in focus. Street setting: modern city, green plants visible, 
natural sunlight. Candid, authentic moment. Raw, editorial photography style. Trendy, cool vibe. 
Soft color grading with earthy tones. Street style magazine quality. Shot on 35mm film aesthetic.
```

**Variants:**
- Change setting: "skateboard park" or "music festival" or "coffee shop"
- Add activity: "sitting on a bench" or "climbing stairs"
- Modify person: "diverse young person" or "person with skateboard"

---

### PROMPT 3: POSTER CONCEPT - "STEPS THAT COUNT"

**Use For:** Campaign poster, print materials, large format displays

```
Bold marketing poster for eco-friendly shoes. Center: Close-up of sustainable sneaker mid-step 
on moss/green terrain. Around it: Flowing graphics showing environmental impact (trees growing, 
water droplets, carbon offset symbols). Color palette: vibrant greens, earth tones, one accent neon color. 
Modern sans-serif typography reading "STEPS THAT COUNT" in bold. Minimalist design, high contrast. 
Campaign poster style. Photographic elements + graphic design blend. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**Variants:**
- Change tagline: "WALK THE TALK" or "YOUR STEPS MATTER"
- Modify impact visuals: "leaves, seedlings, birds"
- Adjust complexity: "simple minimalist" or "complex layered graphics"

---

### PROMPT 4: SOCIAL MEDIA POSTER

**Use For:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest

```
Eye-catching Instagram/TikTok poster. Split design: Left side shows eco-friendly sneaker product shot, 
right side shows environmental impact visualization (trees, water saved, carbon reduced as numbers/graphics). 
Bold typography: "Walk the Talk" in trendy Gen Z font. Vibrant color palette mixing earthy greens with 
one pop of neon. Modern, youthful, sustainable brand aesthetic. Call-to-action tagline. Trending design style. 
1:1 square format OR 9:16 vertical mobile format.
```

**Variants:**
- Change layout: "diagonal split" or "center shoe with rings of data around"
- Modify stats: "500 trees planted, 100L water saved, 50kg carbon offset"
- Adjust font: "graffiti-inspired" or "clean geometric font"

---

### PROMPT 5: FULL CAMPAIGN POSTER

**Use For:** Major campaign launch, billboards, large installations

```
Large-format campaign poster featuring young diverse people (teens to young adults) wearing eco-friendly 
sneakers in various settings: skateboarding, street walking, outdoor activism, everyday moments. 
Shoes are the hero elements. Background: moody urban + nature blend. Typography: "Every Step Shapes Tomorrow" 
in bold, modern font. Color grading: natural, slightly muted with vibrant accent colors. Documentary 
photography meets graphic design. Magazine editorial poster quality. Multiple figures, dynamic composition.
```

**Variants:**
- Change diversity/representation as needed
- Modify settings: "festival, campus, park, marketplace"
- Adjust composition: "grid layout" or "organic scattered layout"

---

### PROMPT 6: MINIMALIST POSTER

**Use For:** Premium positioning, luxury sustainable brand messaging, print ads

```
Minimalist poster design. Single eco-friendly sneaker shown in profile against gradient background 
(green to earth tone). Footprint trail of small plants/leaves beneath shoe. Bold sans-serif text: 
"STEPS THAT COUNT" above, smaller text "Walk Your Values" below. Clean, modern aesthetic. 
Lots of negative space. Scandinavian design influence. Sustainable brand luxury feel. 
High-end minimalist poster design. 11x17 inch poster format.
```

**Variants:**
- Change shoe angle: "top-down view" or "front facing"
- Modify tagline: "Wear Your Values" or "Sustainable Step Forward"
- Adjust color: "monochrome green" or "gradient green to cream"

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Image Dimensions

| Format | Dimensions | Aspect Ratio | Use Case |
|--------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Square (Social) | 1080 x 1080px | 1:1 | Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook |
| Vertical (Mobile) | 1080 x 1350px | 9:16 | Instagram Stories, TikTok, Reels |
| Horizontal (Web) | 1920 x 1080px | 16:9 | Website hero, YouTube, ads |
| Poster | 2400 x 3600px | 2:3 | Print posters, large format |
| Wide Banner | 2400 x 800px | 3:1 | Website banner, billboard |
| Square Large | 3000 x 3000px | 1:1 | High-res social, print |

### Resolution Guidelines
- **Web/Social:** Minimum 1080px width (72 DPI)
- **Print:** Minimum 2400px width (300 DPI)
- **Large Format:** 3000px width or larger (150+ DPI)

### File Formats
- **Web:** JPG (high quality, 85-90% compression)
- **Social:** JPG or PNG
- **Print:** TIFF or high-quality JPG
- **Versatile:** PNG (with transparency when needed)

## USAGE GUIDELINES

### DO's ✅

- ✅ Use authentic imagery that resonates with Gen Z
- ✅ Show the shoe in lifestyle contexts (not just white background)
- ✅ Emphasize sustainability without being preachy
- ✅ Maintain consistent color palette across all images
- ✅ Include diverse representation of people
- ✅ Use clear, bold typography for messaging
- ✅ Show real environmental impact when possible
- ✅ Mix product shots with lifestyle imagery
- ✅ Keep messaging authentic and relatable

### DON'Ts ❌

- ❌ Avoid overly glossy, corporate aesthetic
- ❌ Don't make it look like generic sportswear
- ❌ Avoid preachy environmental messaging
- ❌ Don't use outdated or cliché eco-imagery (lone tree, Earth hug, etc.)
- ❌ Don't sacrifice style for sustainability message
- ❌ Avoid low-quality or blurry imagery
- ❌ Don't use stock photos that look obviously staged
- ❌ Avoid clashing colors; stick to brand palette
- ❌ Don't oversell—let the shoe speak for itself

## CUSTOMIZATION TIPS

### For Different Platforms

**Instagram Feed:**
- Use Prompts 1, 2, 4
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 or 4:5
- Bold colors, clean composition
- Include captions with campaign hashtags

**TikTok/Reels:**
- Use Prompt 2 or 5
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Dynamic, movement-focused
- Shorter text, punchy messaging

**Website Hero:**
- Use Prompt 1 or 3
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- High resolution (1920x1080 minimum)
- Feature product prominently

**Print/Posters:**
- Use Prompts 3, 5, or 6
- High resolution (2400px minimum)
- Professional typography
- Bleed space for printing (add 0.25" on all sides)

**Influencer Content:**
- Use Prompt 2 or 5
- Authentic, unpolished aesthetic
- People wearing shoes in real settings
- Room for captions and stickers

### Prompt Optimization Tips

1. **Add Style References:**
   - "in the style of [magazine] photography"
   - "inspired by [aesthetic] design"
   - "shot like [filmmaker/photographer]"

2. **Specify Technical Details:**
   - "shot on 35mm film"
   - "professional studio lighting"
   - "cinematic color grading"
   - "8K resolution"

3. **Use Aspect Ratio Syntax:**
   - Midjourney: `--ar 16:9` or `--ar 1:1`
   - DALL-E: Specify in prompt or settings
   - Other tools: Check documentation

4. **Iterate & Refine:**
   - Generate 3-4 versions of each prompt
   - Mix and match successful elements
   - Adjust colors, settings, people as needed

5. **Add Negative Prompts:**
   - "avoid: watermark, text, logo"
   - "no blur, no distortion, sharp focus"
   - "not: generic, corporate, dated"
