Eco Friendly Shoes are made from sustainable materials like recycled ocean plastic, plant-based vegan leather, organic cotton, and natural cork soles. Each shoe diverts 500g–1kg of ocean plastic from landfills and saves 50–100 liters of water compared to traditional sneaker production. We are committed to transparency, ethical labor, and measurable environmental regeneration.

Our prices range from $79 to $169 depending on the collection. Entry-level sneakers (Urban Essentials) start at $79–$99, mid-range options (Street Style & Eco Classics) are $89–$129, and premium outdoor/athletic shoes (Trail Explorer & Athletic Performance) are $119–$169.
