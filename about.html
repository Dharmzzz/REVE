The Genesis

Built for the Generation That Walks Its Values

Rêve Eco was founded with a singular conviction: the footwear industry didn't need another generic sneaker brand — it needed a fundamental revolution in how shoes are conceived, sourced, and returned to the earth.

Every year, over 24 billion pairs of synthetic shoes are produced worldwide, the vast majority ending up in landfills where petro-chemical plastics take centuries to decompose. We set out to prove that eco-conscious design doesn't require compromise.

"Every step is either a footprint on the planet or a step towards healing it. We choose to heal."

From the skate parks to the mountain trails, from university campuses to climate marches, Rêve Eco is worn by changemakers united by optimism and radical authenticity.

Ingredient Transparency

What Goes Into Each Shoe

Every material is meticulously selected for maximum circularity, performance, and biodegradability.

Upper Construction

Materials Used

12 post-consumer plastic bottles recycled per pair, organic cotton canvas, and vegetable-tanned vegan leather alternatives.

Environmental Benefit

75% reduction in petroleum reliance vs virgin polyester. Diverts ocean plastics and prevents microplastic landfill runoff.

Plant-Based Soles

Materials Used

Ground cork oak bark harvested non-destructively, blended with renewable tapped rubber and natural latex binder.

Environmental Benefit

Harvested cork oak forests absorb 3–5× more carbon dioxide than unharvested trees. Fully biodegrades at end of lifecycle.

Interior Lining

Materials Used

GOTS-certified (Global Organic Textile Standard) jersey knit cotton combined with breathable recycled foam padding.

Environmental Benefit

Zero synthetic toxic pesticides, 91% less water consumption, hypoallergenic, and naturally odor-inhibiting.

Plantable Packaging

Materials Used

100% post-consumer unbleached cardboard box, water-based inks, and wildflower seed paper labels.

Environmental Benefit

Zero plastic wrapping. Plant your shoe tag in soil, add water, and watch native bee-friendly wildflowers bloom!
